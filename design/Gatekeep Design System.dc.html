gatekeep Design System · v1.0

Quiet, dense, exact. An interface for people who read JWTs for fun.

Neutral warm-grey canvas, one signal colour — Gate jade — reserved for primary actions, "allowed" states and focus. Status colours only ever describe state. Mono is used for identifiers, codes and anything a developer might copy.
